### Phase 3 decision: where the AI endpoint lives (OQ #1)

Went through every hosting option: Directus endpoint, hook + job row, Flow, companion
service, serverless, EAS API routes, and calling the AI from the app (already ruled
out). The choice that matters is sync vs async, not where the code runs. **Decided:
an async `estimate_requests` job row in Directus**, with a sweeper for stuck jobs and
progressive per-tier results. pg-boss is the upgrade path. Full design in BACKEND.md
"Estimation architecture".

### Phase 3 decision: model and price grounding (OQ #2)

Compared Claude tiers, free model tiers (Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral), free
search APIs (Tavily, Exa, Brave) and a local model on the devbox's GTX 1650. A Claude
subscription can't power the backend, so the maintainer chose **free while building**:
Gemini Flash free tier + Tavily, behind a provider-swappable `estimate()`. The launch
model gets picked from a 10-job test. Details in BACKEND.md "Estimation model & price
grounding".
