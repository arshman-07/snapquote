## What we did

### Expo Go timing out → Metro over Tailscale

Expo Go on the phone timed out connecting to Metro, even with the phone and PC on the
same Wi-Fi. Nothing was wrong with the project itself: `tsc`, lint and a full
`expo export --platform android` were all clean. The PC side checked out too. Expo
advertised the correct Wi-Fi IP (`192.168.1.226`), and the Windows Firewall allows
`node.exe` on the Public profile. That leaves the network between phone and PC
(likely client isolation on the "Home 2" Wi-Fi) as the cause.

Rather than fight the router, the app now runs over Tailscale. The PC, the devbox and
the phone are all on the tailnet. A **gitignored `.env.local`** (Expo loads it on top of
`.env`) sets:

| Variable | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `REACT_NATIVE_PACKAGER_HOSTNAME` | `100.73.16.39` (PC) | Metro advertises the Tailscale IP to Expo Go |
| `EXPO_PUBLIC_DIRECTUS_URL` | `http://100.64.144.41:8056` (devbox) | the app can't reach `192.168.1.116` from the phone either |

`.env` is unchanged, and deleting `.env.local` reverts to the LAN addresses.
Consequences:
- **Tailscale must be on, on both phone and PC**, whenever testing.
- Start with `npx expo start -c`, because `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` values are inlined at bundle time.
- Scan the fresh QR code. Expo Go's "recent" entry still points at the old LAN URL.

`npx expo install --check` also flags `expo`, `expo-router`, `expo-linking` and
`expo-glass-effect` one patch behind SDK 57's expected versions. That's not the cause,
and they're left as-is for now.
